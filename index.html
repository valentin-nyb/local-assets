// Cameras

Record every set. Get the clips automatically.

Each OBSBOT Tail 2 streams over the venue's Wi-Fi or network straight to your cloud. When the set ends, the recording is a session for the artist on stage, with vertical clips ready for social media.

Setup and integration included with every camera ordered from us.

  1. 1
    Plug inPower the OBSBOT Tail 2 and connect it to the venue's Wi-Fi or an Ethernet cable.
  2. 2
    Connect itAdd the camera in your dashboard and give it a name. It gets its own stream address and key.
  3. 3
    Connect your calendarPaste your calendar's iCal link. Each event is a set: the title is the DJ, the start and end are the exact times.
  4. 4
    Stream the nightStart the camera before the first set and stop it after the last. Each set is cut at its exact times and saved for its DJ.
  5. 5
    Add your Brand KitUpload logos and fonts for your venue and each artist.
  6. 6
    Set the watermarkPick the logo, position and size, and add the artist's name in one of your fonts. Every clip gets it.
  7. 7
    Choose thumbnailsUse any artwork or photo as a session thumbnail, horizontal and vertical, watermarked.
  8. 8
    PostDownload the clips, or send them to YouTube and SoundCloud.
