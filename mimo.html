Copy your camera's address, then paste it in DJI Mimo.

  1. Open DJI Mimo with the Osmo connected.
  2. Choose Live, then RTMP (custom).
  3. Paste the address and pick the best quality your Wi-Fi allows.
  4. Start the live stream before the first set. It records with the calendar from there.
