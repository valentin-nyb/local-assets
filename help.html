What local / assets™ does

local / assets™ turns every DJ set at a venue into content, automatically. Cameras switch on with your calendar, every set is recorded and saved for the DJ who played it, and the platform makes the rest: the full set in 4K, the HQ audio, vertical clips for Instagram, TikTok and Shorts, thumbnails, and uploads to YouTube and SoundCloud.

Nobody has to press a button on the night. You set it up once, and every set after that is handled.

Who it's for

  • Venues: clubs, bars and event spaces. The cameras record every set, and the venue sees every session.
  • Artists: DJs, with their own sets and Brand Kit.
  • Record labels and managements: the sessions and brand files of the artists they work with, in one place.

Each account has its own private storage for its videos. A venue can give an artist access to their own sets only.

What you need

  • A camera that can live-stream (RTMP): OBSBOT, DJI, Sony Alpha and most streaming cameras work, over the venue's Wi-Fi or a network cable. Or add one of our PTZ cameras for £149 a month (12-month minimum), set up for you. Some plans include cameras: see the plans.
  • A calendar of who plays when: a Google or Apple calendar, where each event's title is the DJ and its times are the set's.
  • For the best sound (optional): the mixer's booth output into an iPad or iPhone through a small USB audio interface (about £25), or into a Mac with a™ for Mac. Without it, the camera's own sound is used.
  • A Google account to sign in with.

Getting started

  • Choose a plan on the home page, say whether you're a venue, an artist or a record label, and pay by card through Stripe.
  • Your account is set up once the payment goes through, and a welcome email tells you how to sign in. Sign in at local-assets.com/login with Google, using the email you paid with.
  • Connect your calendar, add your cameras in Camera Control, and upload your logo and fonts to Brand Kit for the watermark.
  • That's it: the next set in the calendar is recorded by itself. Cameras ordered from us are set up for you.

After every set

Each set becomes a session for that artist and date, with:

  • 4K long form: the whole set at the camera's full resolution.
  • HQ audio: the decks' recording, to download or post.
  • Vertical clips: 1080 × 1920, cut at the set's strongest moments and reframed on the DJ. Your plan sets how many.
  • Thumbnails, and your watermark on the clips if you want it.

Post the long form to YouTube and the audio to SoundCloud from your dashboard, or share a clip straight to Instagram, TikTok or WhatsApp from your phone.

Plans and billing

  • One plan for each kind of account: artists start Free (pay only for what you use; clips carry a small local / assets™ mark) or take the Artist plan to remove it. Record labels have the Label plan, and venues the Venue plan, with a camera set up for you. Current prices are on the home page.
  • Live-Set: two cameras, Starlink Mini and a Mac mini with the multi-camera edit, on request.
  • Usage: each plan includes an amount of video stored and streamed. Anything above it is charged at the rates shown in Billing.
  • Payment: by card through Stripe. The monthly fee and any usage are charged on the 1st of each month, and the invoice is emailed to you. Billing in your dashboard shows your plan, usage so far and past invoices.
  • Several rooms, festivals or groups: talk to us.

Cancelling and refunds

  • Cancel at any time in Billing. Your plan stays active to the end of the month you've paid for, and your recordings, clips and Brand Kit stay downloadable until then. Part-months aren't refunded.
  • Plans with hardware we supply (cameras, Mac mini, Starlink Mini) have a 12-month minimum, shown before you sign up. The hardware comes back to us when the plan ends.
  • If you signed up for yourself rather than a business, you can cancel within 14 days for a full refund: email info@local-assets.com.

The full details are in section 7 of our Terms of Service.

Your files and privacy

  • You own everything you record and upload. We only host, process and deliver it to run the service for you.
  • Each account's videos are kept in its own private storage. Videos only play through short-lived links given to someone signed in to that account, and links you share end after 7 days.
  • When an account closes, its data is kept for up to 30 days and then deleted.
  • We don't use tracking or advertising cookies, and we never sell your data. Our Privacy policy has the details.

The apps

  • The website does everything, on any computer.
  • a™ for Mac (local-assets.com/download) adds what only a Mac can do: it streams cameras and the mixer plugged into it, with no OBS needed, and renders the watermarked 4K long form.
  • iPhone and iPad: open local-assets.com in Safari and choose Share → Add to Home Screen. An iPad can also record the decks.

Troubleshooting

  • A camera isn't live, or OBS says "Could not access the stream key": no set is on, so the stream is closed. It opens 2 minutes before a calendar set, while Camera Control is open, or with Create a session.
  • A recording wasn't kept: it was streamed outside a set. Add the set to the calendar (event title = the DJ), or use Create a session.
  • The clips have no sound: the camera's microphone was off. Turn its audio on, or record the decks; then delete the silent clip and add it back.
  • The mixer doesn't show up on the computer: use the square USB port on the back of the mixer, a cable that carries data, and install the maker's software.
  • A shared link says it has ended: links last 7 days. Make a new one with Copy link.
  • I can't sign in: sign in with the Google account for the email on your account (the one you paid with). If it still doesn't work, email us.
