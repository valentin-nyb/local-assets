Getting started

What local / assets does

local / assets turns every DJ set at a venue into content, automatically. Cameras at the venue switch on with your calendar, every set is recorded and saved for the DJ who played it, and the platform makes the rest: the full set in 4K, the HQ audio, vertical clips ready for Instagram, TikTok and Shorts, thumbnails, and the uploads to YouTube and SoundCloud.

Calendar→Cameras + decks record→Session saved for the DJ→Clips, audio, thumbnails→Watermark→Post or share

Nobody has to press a button on the night. You set it up once, and every set after that is handled.

Getting started

Accounts and who sees what

There are four kinds of account, and each has its own private storage for its videos:

  • Venue: a club, bar or event space. Its cameras record every set, and it sees every session.
  • Artist: a DJ, with their own sets and Brand Kit.
  • Record label: releases music for its artists, and keeps their sessions and brand files together.
  • Management: looks after artists and their content.

Signing in

You sign in with Google, using the email on the account (local-assets.com/login). A sign-in lasts while you use it and ends after a while away, for safety.

Giving an artist access

In Brand Kit, an artist's folder has Give access. Type their email: they sign in with Google and see only their own sets, the calendar and their own Brand Kit folder. They can watch and download, but not change the account.

The apps: website, a™ for Mac, phone and iPad

  • The website (local-assets.com) does everything, on any computer.
  • a™ for Mac (local-assets.com/download) is the same platform as an app, plus things only a Mac can do: it streams cameras and the mixer plugged into the Mac (no OBS needed), and it renders the 4K long form with your moving watermark. It tells you when there's an update.
  • iPhone and iPad: open local-assets.com in Safari and choose Share → Add to Home Screen. An iPad can also record the decks (see Decks on an iPad).

Getting started

Theme and accent colour

At the bottom of the side menu: Light / Dark / Auto, and the coloured dots for the accent colour, the colour of buttons, links, the active menu item, the logo and the loading text. Tap a dot to try it, then Save colour: it's kept with your sign-in, so every device you use shows it.

Recording

The calendar: who plays when

The calendar decides what's recorded and who it belongs to. Connect the venue's Google or Apple calendar once, and every event becomes a set.

  1. In Google Calendar: Settings → your calendar → Integrate calendar → Secret address in iCal format. In Apple Calendar: share the calendar publicly and copy its link.
  2. Open Calendar in local / assets, and paste the link.
  3. Add your nights as events: the event title is the DJ's name, and the start and end are the set's exact times.

The cameras and the decks open 2 minutes before a set and close 2 minutes after. What's kept is cut at the set's exact times and saved for that DJ, so back-to-back sets become separate sessions.

Good to know

Anything streamed outside a set isn't kept (it would cost storage for nothing). To record without a calendar event, use Create a session.

Recording

Cameras

Each camera has its own slot in Camera Control, with a stream address and key. A camera streams over the venue's Wi-Fi or a network cable straight to your storage, and the platform does the rest.

Adding a camera

  1. Power the camera and connect it to the venue's Wi-Fi or a network cable.
  2. In Camera Control, add a camera and give it a name (for example Main floor).
  3. Copy its stream address and stream key into the camera's live-streaming settings (OBSBOT, DJI, OBS, or any camera or app that streams RTMP).
  4. Leave it on. It records whenever a set is on.

Cameras ordered from us (OBSBOT Tail 2, bought or hired) are set up for you. Pro includes one camera and Live-Set two.

Cameras on a Mac (no OBS)

With a™ for Mac open, Camera Control shows This Mac: link a camera or capture card plugged into the Mac to a slot, and a™ streams it. It waits while no set is on and starts by itself when one opens.

Camera Control on your phone

See every camera live, and move, zoom and follow the DJ on cameras that support it (presets and tracking on PTZ cameras).

Sound from the camera

A camera records the sound it hears through its microphone or audio input. For the music itself, use the decks: if a camera sends silence (its microphone off), the clips and the long form take the decks' sound instead.

"Could not access the stream key"

OBS or a camera says this when no set is on: the stream is closed to save cost. It opens 2 minutes before a calendar set, while Camera Control is open somewhere, or with Create a session.

Recording

Create a session (recording by hand)

For a set that isn't in the calendar, press Create a session in Calendar: pick the cameras and the decks, type the artist, and everything starts recording together. Press Stop and save at the end. The recordings are saved as one session for that artist and date.

A set in the calendar that finishes early can be ended with Stop set, so nothing more is kept until its time is over.

The decks

Recording the decks: the ways to do it

The decks' sound becomes each session's HQ audio, and the sound of its clips and long form. There are four ways to get it in. Pick one:

Whichever way, the decks start and stop with the sets, exactly like the cameras, and each recording notes the moment it started, so its sound lines up with the picture.

The decks

Decks on an iPad or iPhone

Mixer booth out→USB audio interface→USB hub→iPad
  1. Plug the mixer's booth out (red and white RCA) into a USB audio interface's inputs. A Behringer UCA202 or UCA222 (about £25) works with no driver.
  2. Plug the interface into a USB hub's rectangular USB-A port, and the hub into the iPad.
  3. On the iPad open local-assets.com → Calendar → Decks audio, and pick USB Audio CODEC.
  4. Press Turn on. It records every set in the calendar, and every Create a session, by itself.
  5. Keep the iPad plugged in and the page open for the night.

The sound is kept on the iPad while it records (a dropped connection loses nothing) and uploads as the session's HQ audio when the set ends. Set the booth level so the meter peaks around −6 dB and never touches red.

The decks

Connecting a mixer by USB (Pioneer XDJ-RX2 and others)

Many mixers and all-in-one systems send their sound over USB, like a sound card:

  1. Use the square USB port on the back of the mixer (the one on top is for USB sticks with music).
  2. Use a cable that carries data (many USB-C cables only charge), plugged straight into the computer or into a hub's USB-A port.
  3. On a Mac: System Settings → Sound → Input should show the mixer. If it doesn't, install the maker's Mac software (pioneerdj.com → your model → Support → Software & firmware) and restart.
  4. Choose the mixer as the sound input (in a™, OBS, or on the iPad).
Watch out

The USB-C port at the end of slim hubs (Satechi and similar) is often the charging input: it passes power but no data, so the computer never sees the mixer. And a red-and-white cable into the computer's headphone socket shows as headphones (an output), not as an input. Use an audio interface instead.

The decks

Decks with OBS

  1. In Calendar, add Decks audio and copy its stream address and key.
  2. In OBS: Settings → Stream → Custom, paste the address and key.
  3. Add an Audio Input Capture source and set its device to the mixer or audio interface (not Default, which is usually a microphone).
  4. Play a track: the green meter in OBS's Audio Mixer should move.
  5. Press Start Streaming. Outside a set OBS is refused (the stream is closed), so leave it running or start it when the set opens.

The decks

Decks with Pioneer DJM-REC

DJM-REC is Pioneer DJ's phone app for recording and streaming a mix. It works with certain Pioneer mixers: DJM-V10, DJM-V10-LF, DJM-V5, DJM-A9, DJM-900NXS2, DJM-750MK2, DJM-450 and the XDJ-AN (not the XDJ-RX2). Pioneer doesn't let other apps connect to it, but it can stream to Restream, which can pass the stream on to us:

Pioneer mixer→DJM-REC on a phone→Restream→Your Decks slot
  1. Connect the mixer to the phone and set up DJM-REC as Pioneer explains.
  2. In Restream, add a custom RTMP destination with your Decks slot's stream address and key (Calendar → Decks audio).
  3. In DJM-REC, stream to Restream.
Before you choose this

It adds a paid Restream plan (custom destinations aren't on every plan) and DJM-REC's own price after its trial, a few seconds' delay, and the phone's camera picture with it. For most venues an iPad with an audio interface is simpler, lossless and free.

The decks

How sound and picture stay together

The cameras and the decks are separate recordings. Each notes the exact moment its first second was recorded, and the clips and long form use that to put the decks' sound at the right moment of the picture. Recordings made before this was added are lined up by their ends instead (stopping a session ends both together).

If a clip's sound is still out, delete the clip and add it back: it's cut again with the latest timing.

After the set

Sessions and Distribution History

Every set becomes a session: an artist and a date. Overview lists them in Distribution History, newest first, each with:

  • 4K Long Form: the whole set, at the camera's full resolution.
  • HQ Audio: the decks' recording, as an audio file to download or post.
  • Vertical clips: 1080 × 1920, ready for Instagram, TikTok and Shorts.
  • Thumbnails: stills of the set (and your own, from Brand Kit).

The latest session banner at the top of Overview loops a few seconds of the set's best moment (found from the music's drops and the movement on stage). Choose moment picks another.

You can also upload a recording yourself in Upload Asset: a 4K long form, and the HQ audio, vertical clips and thumbnails are made from it.

After the set

Vertical clips

Clips are cut automatically from the long form at the set's strongest moments, reframed on the DJ, in your colour look. Your plan sets how many each session gets (Live-Set: unlimited).

  • Choose moments: pick the exact moments yourself; they replace the clips.
  • Delete a clip, and add it back with the + button (it's cut again).
  • Upload your own vertical clips to a session (they're kept as they are, and can be watermarked).
  • Apply watermark stamps your Brand Kit watermark on chosen clips (a GIF logo moves). Remove watermark brings back the clean ones.

The clips take the session's decks sound when the camera recorded none.

After the set

The 4K long form

Play it, download it, or post it. If the camera recorded no sound, the player plays the decks' recording with it, and a copy with the sound inside (for downloads) is made on a Mac with a™.

Watermarking the long form (a™ for Mac)

A 2-hour 4K set is too long to redraw on the servers, so it's done by a™ for Mac, with the Mac's video hardware:

  1. Open the session in a™ for Mac and press Apply watermark on the long form. (On the website it says Watermark in a™ for Mac.)
  2. That Mac starts straight away: it draws your watermark at full resolution (a GIF logo moves) and puts the decks' sound in if the camera recorded none.
  3. The row shows Rendering on [your Mac] · %, then Uploading, then Watermarked. The watermarked copy is played and downloaded in place of the original, which is kept.
Keep a™ open

It takes about as long as the set, plus the upload, and needs about 30 to 40 GB free on the Mac's disk while it works. Remove watermark brings back the original.

After the set

Multi-camera edits (Live-Set)

With two or more cameras, the venue's Mac mini edits every set into one video, cutting between the cameras in time with the music (every 4 to 16 bars), with the decks' sound. It runs after the set and the edit appears with the session.

Brand Kit

Brand Kit

Logos, fonts, artwork, covers, photos, guidelines, videos, GIF animations, Instagram posts and colour looks (.cube LUTs), kept for the account and for each artist, venue and record label you work with.

  • Upload into the right folder and type. A file's type tag changes its type.
  • Drag a file onto another kit to copy it there (the kit lights up in your accent colour).
  • Use as profile photo, Use as watermark, Set as thumbnail, and Use as LUT (the colour look for every vertical clip) are on the files themselves.
  • Videos get a watermarked copy to download.
  • Copy link makes a link to one file that works for 7 days, to send a logo or press photo to a promoter.
  • Download all saves a whole kit as a zip.

Brand Kit

The watermark

Set it up once in Brand Kit → Watermark: the logo (bottom left, or a moving GIF from Animations), the artist's name in one of your fonts (bottom right), position, size and opacity. The preview shows a vertical and a horizontal frame. Each artist can have their own.

It's applied to vertical clips, thumbnails and Brand Kit videos on the website, and to the 4K long form in a™ for Mac. Export PNG saves it as an image.

Publishing

YouTube and SoundCloud

Connect your channels once, then post a session straight from Distribution History: the long form to YouTube (with a thumbnail) and the HQ audio to SoundCloud. A posted session shows On YouTube and On SoundCloud, linking to it.

Publishing

Sharing and downloading

  • On a phone: open a clip and tap Share to send the video itself to Instagram (Story or Reel), TikTok, WhatsApp or any app.
  • On a computer: Share shows a QR code: scan it with your phone to open the clip there, then share it.
  • Copy link makes a link to one clip or file that anyone can open, for 7 days. After that it stops; make a new one.
  • Downloads come through a link that lasts 15 minutes and is noted for the account.

Account

Keeping your files safe

  • Private storage: each account's videos are kept in its own storage, and Brand Kit files in private storage.
  • Signed links: a video, thumbnail or file only plays or opens through a link that lasts a short time and is only given to someone signed in to that account. A link copied from the browser stops working within hours.
  • Only on local-assets.com: videos don't play when embedded on other websites.
  • Shared links end after 7 days.
  • Downloads are noted: who took which file, and when.
  • No right-click menu in the app, and sign-ins end after a while away.
  • The watermark is the answer to screen recording: no website can stop someone filming a screen, but a watermarked clip is always traceable to you.

Help

Troubleshooting

A camera isn't live, or OBS says "Could not access the stream key"

No set is on, so the stream is closed. It opens 2 minutes before a calendar set, while Camera Control is open, or with Create a session.

A recording wasn't kept

It was streamed outside a set. Add the set to the calendar (title = the DJ), or use Create a session.

The clips or the long form have no sound

The camera's microphone was off. Turn its audio on, or record the decks: the clips then take the decks' sound. Delete a silent clip and add it back to cut it again with sound.

The mixer doesn't show up on the computer

Check the cable carries data, use the mixer's square USB port on the back, avoid a hub's charging port, and install the maker's software. See Connecting a mixer by USB.

The banner doesn't play on an iPhone or iPad

Low Power Mode stops videos playing by themselves on every website. The banner shows a still and plays at the first touch; turning Low Power Mode off brings the motion back.

A shared link says it has ended

Links work for 7 days. Make a new one with Copy link.

a™ for Mac shows an error on opening

Install the latest a™ from local-assets.com/download (quit a™ and eject its disk first).
