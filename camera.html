Where do I find the iCal link?
Google Calendar
  1. Open Google Calendar on a computer and go to Settings.
  2. Under Settings for my calendars, pick the calendar.
  3. Open Integrate calendar and copy the Secret address in iCal format.
Apple Calendar
  1. Right-click the calendar and choose Share Calendar.
  2. Tick Public Calendar.
  3. Copy the link (it starts with webcal://).
